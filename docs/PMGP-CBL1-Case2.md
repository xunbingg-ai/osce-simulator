### Part 1: Diagnosis & Differential Diagnosis

**Q1: Write a summary statement and present your diagnosis and differential diagnoses, ranked by likelihood.**

> 请撰写一份 summary statement，并按可能性高低列出你的诊断及鉴别诊断。

**Reference Summary Statement:**

Mr. Chen is a 55-year-old previously healthy male who presents with fatigue for two months. He also notes palpitations with exertion and intermittent bright red blood per rectum after bowel movements. PE is notable for tachycardia and pale conjunctivae. The working diagnosis is Anemia, most likely due to excessive blood loss from GI tract. Initial work-up includes CBC with peripheral smear, iron studies, B12 and folate. Given his age, he should be screened for colorectal cancer. DDx include OSAS, coronary artery disease and depression. ASCVD risk, including lipid panel and glucose, and PHQ-2/9 screening are warranted. If initial work-up doesn't result in a diagnosis, further work-up like stress test and sleep study should be considered.

> 陈先生是一位 55 岁既往健康的男性，主诉进行性加重的乏力两个月。伴随劳力性心悸，及大便后间断鲜红色血便。体格检查发现心动过速、结膜苍白。初步诊断为贫血，最可能由消化道失血引起。初始检查包括全血细胞计数及外周血涂片、铁代谢检查、维生素 B12 和叶酸水平。鉴于患者年龄，需筛查结直肠癌。鉴别诊断包括阻塞性睡眠呼吸暂停、冠心病及抑郁症。还需评估 ASCVD 风险（包括血脂和血糖），并做 PHQ-2/9 筛查。若初始检查未能明确诊断，应进一步考虑负荷试验和睡眠监测。

---

#### Differential Diagnosis Table（鉴别诊断详表）

**1. Anemia（贫血）— Most Likely（最可能）**

Anemia can cause tachycardia and palpitations which are both present here. Given Mr. Chen's rectal bleeding and finding of pallor, anemia is high on the differential.

> 贫血可以解释患者的心动过速和心悸。结合直肠出血史和体格检查发现的结膜苍白，贫血是最可能的诊断。

**2. Occult Malignancy（隐匿性恶性肿瘤）**

Mr. Chen's report of rectal bleeding could be explained by colorectal cancer, and this needs to be further evaluated.

> 患者的直肠出血可能由结直肠癌引起，需要进一步排查。建议行粪便潜血试验或结肠镜检查。

**3. Obstructive Sleep Apnea（阻塞性睡眠呼吸暂停，OSAS）**

Mr. Chen reports snoring. If another cause is not apparent, further evaluation (such as a sleep study) may be helpful. Even though Mr. Chen notes a concern of easy exhaustion with exertion (while most OSA patients with somnolence report improved wakefulness with activity), it is reasonable to keep OSA on the differential until further examination or lab brings another diagnosis clearly to the fore.

> 患者自述打鼾。若其他病因未明确，应考虑进一步行睡眠监测。尽管患者的疲劳在活动后加重（而大多数 OSA 患者的嗜睡在活动后改善），在实验室检查明确诊断前，OSA 仍应保留在鉴别诊断中。

**4. Coronary Artery Disease（冠心病，CAD）**

Coronary artery disease should be considered in adults with fatigue, especially in patients like Mr. Chen who reports exertional symptoms. He lacks risk factors for CAD other than his age (we do not yet know his cholesterol levels), and the findings suggestive of anemia point towards that diagnosis first. If an initial workup for common causes does not result in a diagnosis, it would be appropriate for Mr. Chen to undergo a stress test. Mr. Chen's easy fatigue with exertion could be an anginal equivalent (a symptom of cardiac ischemia that does not involve chest pain) and would need to be evaluated if no other cause is identified. But it would be unusual for this not to be relieved with rest.

> 对有乏力症状的成人，尤其如本例有劳力性症状的患者，应考虑冠心病。本例除年龄外缺乏其他 CAD 危险因素（血脂水平未知），且提示贫血的证据更为突出。若常规病因筛查未能确诊，可行负荷试验。患者的劳力性疲劳可能是心绞痛等位征（无胸痛表现的心肌缺血症状），若其他病因被排除则需进一步评估。但症状不能通过休息缓解，这一点不符合典型心绞痛。

**5. Depression（抑郁症）**

Mr. Chen does report decreased time for recreational activities although he attributes this to his fatigue. Although the history so far seems to indicate he is not depressed, it will be important to explore this when you go back into the room. PHQ-2 screening is negative.

> 患者自述娱乐活动时间减少，但归因于疲劳而非情绪问题。目前病史不支持抑郁症诊断，但需进一步筛查。PHQ-2 筛查结果为阴性。

---

##### Less Likely Diagnoses（可能性较低的诊断）

**6. Diabetes Mellitus（糖尿病）**

While diabetes can be a cause for fatigue, Mr. Chen lacks signs or symptoms of diabetes (polyuria, polydipsia), has an average BMI of 24, and does not have a family history of diabetes.

> 虽然糖尿病可导致乏力，但患者缺乏典型症状（多尿、多饮），BMI 正常（24 kg/m²），无糖尿病家族史，可能性较低。

**7. Sleep Restriction（睡眠不足）**

Mr. Chen reports no recent changes in his home or work life that could account for his symptoms. Additionally, he reports getting more sleep than usual.

> 患者否认近期家庭或工作变化，且自述睡眠时间较平时更多，不符合睡眠不足。

**8. Hypothyroidism（甲状腺功能减退）**

Mr. Chen does not report usual associated symptoms such as weight gain, cold intolerance, constipation, or depression. Additionally, the prevalence of hypothyroidism is ten times higher among females than males, making it less likely in this case.

> 患者无甲减常见伴随症状（体重增加、畏寒、便秘、抑郁）。且甲减在女性中的患病率是男性的十倍，本病例可能性较低。

**9. Chronic Fatigue Syndrome（慢性疲劳综合征）**

Mr. Chen does not meet the diagnostic criteria since he lacks any of the cardinal symptoms. Furthermore, this is a diagnosis of exclusion, and other causes of his fatigue have not been ruled out.

> 患者不符合 CFS 诊断标准，缺乏核心症状。且此为排除性诊断，目前尚未排除其他病因。

### Part 5: Other VIVA Questions

**Q5: What are the common causes of iron deficiency anemia?**

- Chronic blood loss (GI — most common in adult males and postmenopausal women, menstrual in premenopausal women)
- Decreased iron absorption (gastrectomy, celiac disease, PPI overuse)
- Increased iron demand (pregnancy, growth spurts in children)
- Poor dietary intake (rare as sole cause)

**Q6: How do you differentiate iron deficiency anemia from anemia of chronic disease on lab tests?**

| Parameter | Iron Deficiency | Anemia of Chronic Disease |
|---|---|---|
| Serum iron | ↓ | ↓ |
| Ferritin | ↓ | ↑ or normal |
| TIBC | ↑ | ↓ or normal |
| Transferrin saturation | ↓ | ↓ |
| sTfR (soluble transferrin receptor) | ↑ | Normal |

**Q7: What would you say to Mr. Chen regarding his refusal of colonoscopy?**

- Acknowledge his belief that it's "just hemorrhoids" — validate his perspective
- Explain that at age 55 with new/worsening rectal bleeding, guidelines recommend colorectal cancer screening
- Emphasize that most hemorrhoids do NOT require a colonoscopy, but the purpose here is to rule out more serious causes
- Discuss alternatives: fecal immunochemical test (FIT) as a less invasive option
- Respect patient autonomy — document the refusal and arrange follow-up
